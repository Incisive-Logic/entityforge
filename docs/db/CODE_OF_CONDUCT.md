# Community conduct

EntityForgeDB welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds. Be respectful, discuss ideas
and evidence rather than individuals, assume good intent while accepting correction, and avoid harassment,
threats, discrimination or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community or
project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact or
security-reporting facility. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
